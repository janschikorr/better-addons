# Description template

> Status: phase 1 draft. Finalised in phase 9.

An addon description exists so that a player can decide in thirty seconds whether
this addon is for them. Everything else is noise.

## Template

```markdown
# <Addon name>

<One sentence: what it does, in the player's words.>

## What it does

- <concrete capability>
- <concrete capability>

## What it does not do

- <the thing people will reasonably expect and not find>

## Known limits

- <what breaks, where, and whether a fix is planned>

## Requires

- <other addons or libraries, with versions>

## Screenshots

<Real screenshots from the game, showing the addon in use. No mock-ups.>
```

## Rules

- **No emoji in headings or lists.** One in the title at most, if the project has a
  mark of its own.
- **No superlatives.** Not "the best", not "the ultimate", not "blazing fast".
- **No features that do not exist yet.** A roadmap belongs under a roadmap heading,
  phrased as a plan, not as a capability.
- **"What it does not do" is mandatory.** It is the section that saves support
  effort, and the clearest signal that someone actually used the addon.
- **Screenshots are real.** From the game, at normal UI scale, showing the addon
  doing the thing the description claims.
- **Name the source for bundled data**, with its licence.
